Digital Teaching Toolkit

A Microlearning Website for Basic School Teachers' Effective Integration of Educational Technology

Learn how to select, use and evaluate digital technologies to support effective and learner-centred teaching.

Start Learning

About the Toolkit

The Digital Teaching Toolkit is designed to support basic school teachers in developing practical skills for integrating educational technology into classroom teaching.

The website uses short learning modules, practical activities, scenarios and quizzes to help teachers make informed decisions about when, why and how to use technology in teaching.

What You Will Learn

Understand

Explain the meaning and importance of educational technology integration.

Select

Select appropriate digital tools based on learning objectives and learner needs.

Apply

Design simple technology-supported learning activities.

Evaluate

Evaluate digital resources for usefulness, accessibility and responsible use.
