Digital Tool Evaluation Checklist

  • Does the tool support my learning objective?
  • Can my learners access it?
  • Is it easy to use?
  • Is it accessible?
  • Does it require internet access?
  • Does it protect learner information?
  • Is it affordable?

Technology-Supported Lesson Planning

Use these questions when planning a technology-supported lesson.

  1. What should learners achieve?
  2. What technology will support the objective?
  3. How will learners use the technology?
  4. How will I assess learning?
  5. What will I do if the technology fails?

Accessibility Checklist

  • Use readable text.
  • Provide alternative text for important images.
  • Use captions for videos.
  • Use clear headings.
  • Provide simple instructions.
  • Avoid unnecessary animations.
